schema Pipeline & Processing

  • • Audio Input: Sustained phonation audio (sr=22.05 kHz).
  • • Feature Extraction: 40 Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (MFCCs) framed into 162 time steps (Shape: 162 × 40).
  • • Convolutional Block: Conv2D layers extract spatial acoustic frequency textures.
  • • BiLSTM Layer: Bidirectional Long Short-Term Memory models temporal pitch and micro-tremor variations over time.
  • • Classification: Dense Sigmoid layer outputting Parkinson's probability (0 = Healthy, 1 = Parkinson's).
